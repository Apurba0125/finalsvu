Why Choose Us

Welcome to Swami Vivekananda University. Established in 2019, it is among the most promising and upcoming private universities in Eastern India. With more than 10,000 students, 500 research scholars and 300 faculty members, it has always been the university's endeavour to transform bold ambitions of the future generations into life-changing realities. Under the guidance of eminent national and international scholars as mentors, the university is well defined by the spirit of innovation and enterprise. Students are provided with fully air conditioned classrooms, a high-quality learning environment with smart classrooms, an extensive library with digital resources, well-equipped laboratories with modern pedagogical tools, a hybrid mode of education system, ICT based teaching methods, and highly qualified, well-trained and encouraging educators. The university provides a wide range of diploma, graduation and post-graduation courses along with doctoral degree programmes in multiple disciplines.

The aesthetic campus forms the centre of attraction for the students as it includes greeneries and water bodies, and along with that, it is also furnished with a gymnasium, cafeteria, open air theatre, auditorium, and all other possible amenities of the modern times. Most importantly, the university also facilitates a number of scholarships to help students financially to pursue their dreams.

Mission & Vision

Swami Vivekananda University was founded to widen access to higher education in Eastern India without lowering what that education demands. The mission works out as:

  • Education: to deliver diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes that hold their value in the workplace, taught by faculty who are still active in their own fields.

  • Research: to build a research culture that reaches undergraduates, not only scholars — supported by funded laboratories and departmental research groups.

  • Industry connection: to keep curricula answerable to what employers actually need, through partnerships, internships and professional bodies.

  • Access: to make sure a capable student is never turned away by cost, through scholarships and financial assistance.

Our Core Values

The values that shape how the university teaches, researches and grows:

  • Excellence: holding teaching, assessment and research to a standard that stands up outside the campus gates.

  • Innovation: treating new methods, tools and ideas as the normal way of working rather than an occasional experiment.

  • Integrity: honest evaluation, honest research and honest dealing with students and their families.

  • Inclusion: a campus where background, language and means do not decide how far a student gets.

Salient Features

What a student actually finds on arrival:

  • Fully air conditioned smart classrooms with ICT based teaching and a hybrid mode of delivery.

  • An extensive library with digital resources, alongside laboratories equipped with modern pedagogical tools.

  • A green campus with water bodies, a gymnasium, cafeteria, open air theatre and auditorium.

  • Diploma through doctoral programmes across engineering, science, management, law, allied health and the humanities.

Awards & Recognitions

We are elated to share the list of the prestigious awards that we have won in different categories over the past years.

Swami Vivekananda University won the coveted News18 Bangla Education Eminence Award 2024 for Most Promising University in Eastern India on 21st June 2024.

The News18 Bangla Education Eminence Award 2024 honours outstanding contributions to the field of education. Recognising institutions and individuals who demonstrate exceptional academic excellence, innovative teaching methods, and impactful community engagement, this prestigious award highlights those who drive educational advancement and inspire future leaders.

Swami Vivekananda University won the prestigious Zee Education Excellence Award 2024 for Excellence in Academic Infrastructure & Activities on 26th June 2024.

The Zee Education Excellence Award 2024 celebrates outstanding achievements in the field of education. Presented by Zee Media, it honours institutions, educators and innovators who demonstrate exceptional quality in teaching, research and educational leadership.

Swami Vivekananda University won the esteemed ABP Ananda Shiksha Samman 2024 for Excellence in Academic Infrastructure on 27th June 2024.

The ABP Ananda Shiksha Samman 2024 recognises exceptional achievements in education. Presented by ABP Ananda, it honours institutions and educators who have made significant contributions to advancing educational standards and practices.

Swami Vivekananda Group of Institutes won the prestigious ABP Ananda Shiksha Samman 2023 for Outstanding Academic Excellence on 10th June 2023.

The award celebrates excellence in teaching, innovative educational methods and impactful community initiatives — a mark of distinction for those shaping the future of education with commitment.

Swami Vivekananda University won the prestigious News18 Bangla Education Eminence Award 2022 for Excellence in Academic Performance on 28th May 2022.

Awarded by News18 Bangla, a leading media outlet, it celebrates achievements that elevate educational standards and foster transformative learning experiences.
