Why Choose Us

Welcome to Swami Vivekananda University. Established in 2019, it is among the most promising and upcoming private universities in Eastern India. With more than 10,000 students, 500 research scholars and 300 faculty members, it has always been the university's endeavour to transform bold ambitions of the future generations into life-changing realities. Under the guidance of eminent national and international scholars as mentors, the university is well defined by the spirit of innovation and enterprise. Students are provided with fully air conditioned classrooms, a high-quality learning environment with smart classrooms, an extensive library with digital resources, well-equipped laboratories with modern pedagogical tools, a hybrid mode of education system, ICT based teaching methods, and highly qualified, well-trained and encouraging educators. The university provides a wide range of diploma, graduation and post-graduation courses along with doctoral degree programmes in multiple disciplines.

The aesthetic campus forms the centre of attraction for the students as it includes greeneries and water bodies, and along with that, it is also furnished with a gymnasium, cafeteria, open air theatre, auditorium, and all other possible amenities of the modern times. Most importantly, the university also facilitates a number of scholarships to help students financially to pursue their dreams.

Mission

An institution's mission is a concise statement that expresses its central purpose and main objectives. For our esteemed institution, the mission is primarily a focus on providing quality education, promoting intellectual growth, and preparing students for successful careers. A commitment to academic excellence, student development, and community involvement is always emphasized.

The clear and compelling mission statement of Swami Vivekananda University helps align the institution's efforts and resources toward a common goal and ensures that all members understand and work toward the same goals. Additionally, our purpose is communicated to external stakeholders, including potential students, partners, and the community, helping to build a strong brand and garner support.

Vision

The vision of Swami Vivekananda University is to be a leading institution of higher education that contributes significantly to the advancement of knowledge, research and social development. Its goal is to provide a world-class educational experience that promotes intellectual growth, innovation and leadership among its students and teachers. The university sees itself as a center of academic excellence and a cutting-edge research center addressing current global challenges.

The University makes constant effort to preserve and enhance its legacy of academic achievement and social impact. It aims to provide students with the skills and knowledge they need to excel in their chosen fields and make a meaningful contribution to society. The vision emphasizes a commitment to inclusion, collaboration, and the continued pursuit of excellence in education and research.

By focusing on these areas, Swami Vivekananda University aims to maintain its reputation as a renowned institution with a global perspective, dedicated to fostering a dynamic and progressive learning environment.

Our Core Values

Core values are fundamental principles that define an institution's identity and guide its actions, decisions, and interactions. The core values of Swami Vivekananda University serve as a moral compass and help us create a sense of shared purpose among members. They guide our actions, decisions, and culture. They reflect the beliefs and priorities of the University and shape our identity. The core values of our institution include:

  • Integrity: Maintaining honesty and strict ethical standards.

  • Excellence: Striving for high quality and continuous improvement.

  • Respect: Valuing diversity and treating everyone with dignity.

  • Innovation: Encouraging creativity and new ideas.

  • Responsibility: Taking responsibility for actions and results.

  • Collaboration: Promoting teamwork and partnerships.

  • Service: Committing to serve others and the community.

These values help us define our purpose and guide us to ensure consistency and alignment with our mission. By adhering to these core values, we not only define our culture but also create a solid foundation for achieving our mission and objectives. These values determine and shape our decision-making processes and help us to create a cohesive and purpose-driven organization.

Salient Features

Swami Vivekananda University was founded with the vision to promote holistic education inspired by the teachings of Swami Vivekananda. It has several notable features like:

  • Holistic Education: Emphasizing not only on academic excellence but also on personal development and integrating physical, mental and spiritual growth.

  • Values-based learning: The focus is on providing education based on ethical and moral values and promoting character formation and intellectual growth.

  • Innovative Curriculum: Providing a curriculum that is dynamic and relevant and incorporating contemporary educational practices and research trends.

  • Cultural and Spiritual Enrichment: Providing students with opportunities to participate in cultural activities and spiritual practices, promoting a balanced and enriching learning environment.

  • Community Engagement: Involving students in community service and outreach programs and emphasizing the importance of making a positive contribution to society.

  • State-of-the-art facilities: Equipped with modern infrastructure including advanced laboratories, libraries and digital resources, the university strives to enhance the learning experience.

  • Experienced Faculty: Consisting of a team of dedicated and experienced educators committed to providing quality teaching and mentoring.

These characteristics reflect the University's commitment to educating well-rounded individuals who are not only academically competent but also socially responsible and ethically sound.
