That is the doc_url filter in the view doing the work — a bare {% templatetag openblock %} static {% templatetag closeblock %} tag would raise for a path that was never collected and take the whole page down over one missing PDF. The whole row is the target rather than just the icon, which is what makes it comfortable on a phone — a 24px icon is below the 44px minimum for a touch target.
