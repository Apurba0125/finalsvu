    The Campus

    The university sits at Barrackpore, north of Kolkata, on a green campus built around water bodies and open ground. What follows is what a student actually finds on arrival — the rooms they are taught in, the laboratories they work in, and the places they spend the hours between.

    Smart Classrooms

    Fully air-conditioned lecture rooms with digital boards and projection, wired for the hybrid mode the university teaches in, so a session can be followed in the room or from outside it.

    Laboratories

    Engineering, science and allied-health laboratories mapped to the syllabus rather than to a minimum requirement, and kept open beyond scheduled hours for students working on projects of their own.

    Library & Digital Resources

    A reference and lending library alongside a digital collection of journals, e-books and theses, reachable on campus and off it.

    Open the digital library (opens in a new tab)

    Study & Project Spaces

    Rooms set aside for group work, seminar practice and project building, because a good deal of what a degree teaches happens between students rather than at the front of a room.

    Sports & Gymnasium

    Indoor and outdoor sport with a gymnasium on campus, and ground for the inter-departmental and inter-university fixtures the university takes part in.

    A Green Campus

    Planted grounds and water bodies, an open-air theatre and an auditorium for the cultural calendar — the part of the campus that does not appear on a syllabus and is remembered anyway.
