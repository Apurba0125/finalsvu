THE TWO PANELS The pills and panels are the site's shared tab script — initTabPanels in main.js — which finds them by the data-tabs attribute on the wrapper and the .tab-btn / .tab-pane classes inside it, and are styled by the shared tabs.css. Those names are a contract with the script: rename one and the panel stops switching. Everything else about the block is free. Whichever pill carries is-active in the markup opens on load; without one the script falls back to the first. The ids only have to match between a button's data-tab-target and its panel, so a third panel is a matter of copying a button and a panel and giving the pair a new id.
