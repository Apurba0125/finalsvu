THE FIVE PANELS The pills and panels are the site's shared tab script — initTabPanels in main.js — which finds them by the data-tabs attribute on the wrapper, the .tab-btn and .tab-pane classes inside it, and data-tabs-nav on the pill row. Those names are a contract with the script: rename one and the panels stop switching. Everything inside a panel is free. Whichever pill carries is-active in the markup opens on load; without one the script falls back to the first. The ids only have to match between a button's data-tab-target and its panel, so a sixth panel is a matter of copying a button and a panel and giving the pair a new id. Five pills wrap to three rows on a phone, for panels often shorter than their own navigation, so under 768px they collapse behind the toggle button at the top. That is the same control the department pages use, and it needs no upkeep: the label reads whichever pill is open. NOTE ON COMMENTS: the hash-style Django comment {# … #} covers a SINGLE line.
