THE THREE PANELS The pills and panels are the site's shared tab script — initTabPanels in main.js — which finds them by the data-tabs attribute on the wrapper, the .tab-btn and .tab-pane classes inside it, and data-tabs-nav on the pill row. Those names are a contract with the script: rename one and the panels stop switching. Whichever pill carries is-active opens on load. The ids only have to match between a button's data-tab-target and its panel, so a fourth panel is a matter of copying a button and a panel and giving the pair a new id. Under 768px the pills collapse behind the toggle at the top, whose label reads whichever panel is open.
