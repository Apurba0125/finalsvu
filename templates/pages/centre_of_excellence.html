        NOTICE:

        Applications are invited for the six months’ Certificate Course on Introduction to IBM Business Cognos Analytics under the Centre for Artificial Intelligence, Swami Vivekananda University. The course highlights are as follows:

        • Eligibility: Open to students pursuing Graduation 5th Semester and above
        • Duration: 24 weeks (6 months)
        • Hours / week: 01 Hours per day
        • Mode of Delivery: Hybrid (i.e. both online and on campus)
        • Assessment: Written Examination and Viva-Voce to be held at the end of the course.
        • Certification: Participants who successfully complete the course requirements will duly receive a Certificate on Artificial Intelligence from Swami Vivekananda University.
        • Course Fees: Free

        Apply Online

        COURSE:

        Certificate Course on IBM Cognos Analytics

        CONTENT:

        This course will cover the foundational aspects of Artificial Intelligence, including theoretical concepts and practical applications. The content is structured to provide a step-by-step understanding of AI, from basic principles to more advanced topics.

        Overview of RISC-V

        Week 1: Introduction to RISC-V

        • Overview of RISC-V architecture and its history
        • Comparison with other ISAs (e.g. x86, ARM)
        • RISC-V design goals and benefits
        • Basic components of RISC-V architecture: registers, ALU, and memory

        Week 2: RISC-V Instruction Set

        • RISC-V base integer instructions (RV32I, RV64I)
        • Formats and encoding of RISC-V instructions
        • Addressing modes and immediate values
        • Control and status registers

        Week 3: RISC-V Extensions

        • Overview of standard extensions (e.g. M, A, F, D, Q)
        • Implementing and utilizing extension instructions
        • Custom extensions and their applications
        • Practical exercises with RISC-V extensions

        Week 4: RISC-V Assembly Language Programming

        • Writing and understanding RISC-V assembly code
        • Using simulators and emulators for assembly code testing
        • Debugging techniques for RISC-V assembly programs
        • Hands-on assembly language programming exercises

        Week 5: RISC-V Microarchitecture and Design

        • Overview of RISC-V core microarchitecture
        • Pipeline design and hazards
        • Branch prediction and cache design
        • Implementation of RISC-V cores: single-cycle, multi-cycle, and pipelined designs

        Week 6: RISC-V Toolchain and Development Environment

        • Introduction to RISC-V development tools (e.g. GCC, LLVM)
        • Setting up a RISC-V development environment
        • Cross-compilation and linking for RISC-V
        • Using RISC-V simulators and debuggers (e.g. Spike, QEMU)

        Week 7: Advanced Topics and Applications

        • RISC-V in embedded systems and IoT devices
        • Performance optimization techniques for RISC-V
        • Security features and considerations in RISC-V design
        • Case studies of RISC-V in industry applications

        Week 8: Final Project and Review

        • Project work: designing and implementing a RISC-V based system or application
        • Presentation of final projects and peer review
        • Course review and summary
        • Certification examination and feedback

        MISSION:

        To equip participants with a thorough understanding of the RISC-V instruction set architecture, enabling them to design, implement, and optimize RISC-V-based systems. The course aims to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application, fostering innovation in computer architecture and embedded systems.

        VISION:

        To be a leading educational program in RISC-V architecture, recognized for its excellence in teaching, hands-on training, and contribution to advancing the field of computer engineering. We aspire to create a community of skilled professionals who drive the future of open-source hardware and processor design.

        OBJECTIVES:

        1. Comprehensive Understanding:
          • Provide a deep understanding of the RISC-V ISA, including its core principles, instruction formats, and architectural features.
          • Explore the design goals and benefits of RISC-V compared to other ISAs.
        2. Hands-On Experience:
          • Offer practical training in designing, implementing, and debugging RISC-V processors.
          • Develop proficiency with RISC-V tools, simulators, and development environments.
        3. Skill Development:
          • Teach participants to write and optimize RISC-V assembly language programs.
          • Enable students to apply RISC-V architecture principles in real-world scenarios and embedded systems.
        4. Innovation and Application:
          • Encourage innovative use of RISC-V in various applications, from embedded systems to advanced computing systems.
          • Provide opportunities to work on projects that demonstrate the practical applications of RISC-V.
        5. Professional Growth:
          • Prepare participants for careers in computer architecture, embedded systems, and related fields.
          • Support professional development through a strong focus on industry-relevant skills and knowledge.
        6. Collaboration and Community:
          • Foster a collaborative learning environment where students can engage with peers, instructors, and industry professionals.
          • Build a network of RISC-V enthusiasts and experts who contribute to the advancement of open-source hardware.

        DURATION:

        The entire course shall be of 03 (Three) months’ duration.

        ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:

        Any B.Tech Students who are currently pursuing their final year or pre-final year (are also eligible to apply).

      23. Centre for Excellence by Esillor Luxottica

        Blue filter lenses are designed to improve visual comfort and protect the eyes by filtering out certain high-energy wavelengths of blue light. The primary objectives of blue filter lenses for enhanced vision would aim to:

        1. Reducing Digital Eye Strain:

          Blue filter lenses help minimize eye strain caused by prolonged exposure to screens, such as computers, tablets, and smartphones, by reducing blue light intensity.

        2. Improving Visual Contrast:

          By selectively filtering blue light, these lenses enhance contrast and visual sharpness, making it easier to perceive finer details in low-light or high-glare environments.

        3. Minimizing Glare:

          Blue light contributes significantly to glare, especially in bright outdoor or artificial lighting conditions. Blue filter lenses reduce this glare, improving comfort in various lighting scenarios.

        4. Improving Sleep Patterns:

          Excessive exposure to blue light, particularly in the evening, disrupts circadian rhythms by suppressing melatonin production. Blue filter lenses can help mitigate this disruption, promoting better sleep.

        5. Enhancing Visual Comfort in Indoor Lighting:

          LED and fluorescent lighting emit significant blue light. Blue filter lenses reduce the strain and discomfort caused by these light sources.

        6. Protecting Against Long-Term Eye Damage:

          Prolonged exposure to blue light may contribute to the development of macular degeneration and other eye health issues. Blue filter lenses help protect the eyes from potential long-term damage.

        7. Enhancing Nighttime Vision:

          By reducing glare and filtering out blue light, these lenses can enhance visual clarity during nighttime driving or in low-light conditions, leading to improved safety.
